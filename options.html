Preferred measurement system

UK imperial uses British pints, quarts, gallons and fluid ounces (1 UK gal = 4.55 L, 1 US gal = 3.79 L), shares miles, feet and pounds with US customary, and keeps temperatures in °C as in the UK. Override any category below.

Target units per category

“Auto” picks the best-fitting unit of your preferred system (e.g. cm for small lengths, km for long distances). Choosing a specific unit always converts into it — values already in that unit’s system are left alone.

Advanced: convert each unit individually

Pick exactly what each unit becomes — e.g. miles → kilometres, feet → centimetres, °F → off. A rule always wins over the settings above, even within the same system (km → m). Units left on “Default” follow the category and system settings.
